# Between Sets privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Between Sets is a rest timer. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No account, sign-in or analytics.
- No internet access: the app does not request the internet permission.
- Your timer settings, custom timers and set count stay on your phone, in the app's own storage.
  Uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Permissions and why:**

- **Notifications:** shows the countdown and buttons on the lock screen, and tells you when a rest is over.
- **Alarms (exact alarms):** rings the chime at the exact second the rest ends, even with the phone locked.
- **Run at startup:** puts a running timer back after the phone restarts.
- **Vibration:** buzzes when a rest is over.
